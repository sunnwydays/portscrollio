# Portscrollio (portfolio + scroll)

## Resumes are silly. 

We are still sending single page PDFs based in the ancient 
days of paper. Text just gets skimmed by an algorithm, and if an algorithm can 
do it, the recruiter has no need to. 

A resume no longer catches attention like it should. You could blame that on
skill inflation, but skill isn't the biggest factor either. What's the best way
to get a job? To know somebody. If you know an insider, they have no reason to
reject you: they know you are capable, and even if the skills aren't a perfect 
match, they trust that you can figure it out. You would have to REALLY lack 
experience for them to say no - and in that case, it's for the better.

Unless you are already buddies with an insider, you have to put in the work to
get to know them. Show them some exciting bite-sized videos about your career 
during a coffee chat, and you have non-awkwardly shown them your interest and 
your skill. Even a recruiter glancing at your portfolio site will feel their 
intrigue towards you after hearing you talk like someone they would want to work
with, immeasurably more than after F-scanning your black-and-white A4 PDF.

Those are the thoughts of me, having consumed countless productivity, career,
and tech videos, in long and short form. Great thing is that I can grab details 
from all the social media platforms that have already done the research!

Lastly, is it my skills or is it my application that's holding me back? I don't 
know, but I won't find out without changing it up.

## Recruiters are people too

They have lives, and if they can do their jobs remotely, they will.

I asked: 

```
when recruiters review people's applications and websites, they usually do it 
from their desktop right? I'm having a hard time imagining a recruiter going 
about their workflow from their phone. just for me to think about if i also need 
to prioritize mobile design. but i think i can also copy tiktok anyways there
```

LLM says:

```
While your intuition is mostly right—heavy lifting like detailed technical screening usually happens on a desktop—the "first touch" is increasingly happening on mobile.

Recent data from 2025 and 2026 shows a major shift in how recruitment works. Here is the reality of the recruiter's workflow and how it affects your "Portscrollio."

1. The "Mobile-First" Reality
It’s a mistake to imagine recruiters only sitting at a desk.

The "On-the-Go" Screen: Recruiters at mid-sized companies or agencies are essentially sales reps. They are often checking LinkedIn or their ATS (Applicant Tracking System) between meetings, while commuting, or during lunch.

The First Filter: About 67% of job applications are now completed on mobile, and modern ATS platforms like Greenhouse or Workable have powerful mobile apps. A recruiter might "swipe" through 20 portfolios on their phone to decide which 3 are worth opening on their desktop later for a deep dive.

The "Vibe" Check: Your "For You" page (TikTok style) is native to mobile. If it looks broken on a phone, they’ll assume you can't build a responsive site—which is a red flag for a Computer Engineering student.

2. Desktop is for the "Deep Dive"
Once you pass that 30-second mobile vibe check, the recruiter (or the hiring manager/engineer) will switch to a desktop.
```

    the side, replacing the like and other buttons

    - not all the videos will be relevant - this will keep users scrolling (Skinner box)
    - purpose and impact caption smaller below video title
    - technologies button replaces comment button: you have to click on it 
    (invoke curiousity, encourage interaction) to see the relevant technologies/ 
    skills, stays open pinned (if on desktop) to the right side of the screen 
    (or bottom if mobile) just like the comments section

    - could be more scrolling
    - could even combine with explore

    - youtube homepage style
    - titles trail off so you have to click
4. Profile: bit of resume-style info
    - absolutely should not resemble a resume
    - displays location (Toronto) and school/program (UofT engineering)
    - actually this page may not be necessary - force user to go through videos to get to know me
